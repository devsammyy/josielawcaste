About Us

Josie Lawcastle (JLC) is one of the leading full service law firm in Nigeria, committed to providing zenith legal services in Nigeria, Africa, and globally while meeting the legal needs and demands of our esteemed clients.

The law firm comprises a team of vibrant, innovative, and competent Legal Practitioners who have emerged in this 21st century to provide professional services with little to reservation. Thus, JLC is mindful of modern day demands and the diversity that comes with it.

JLC pioneered in January 2022 is headed and founded by Josephine Aransiola. Our legal team members are spread across the country and outside.

Our Mission

Providing legal solutions; attending to the legal demands of our clients with utmost regard to the precepts of excellence, integrity and professionalism.

Our Vision

To be one of the foremost leading legal services provider in Nigeria, and attain great success at all we do.

Areas of Expertise

We have 3 major pillars of practice which we fondly call, the Big Three (3). They are:

01

Corporate Services

  • Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) services
  • Contract and Legal Document Services
  • Obtainment of licenses (SCUML, NSITF, etc.)
  • Compliance Services (NAFDAC, SONCAP, NITDA)

Additional Services:

  • As an accredited CAC agent, our services cover registration of businesses, incorporations, corporate searches, post incorporation matters, guidance and support to startups and established companies alike.
  • We provide expert drafting and review of business contracts, and preparation of all necessary legal documents to protect your business operations.
  • Employment Onboarding books - We develop all necessary onboarding documents and books your organization needs for employees.
  • Notary Public services - We assist clients get their documents notarized for use within and outside the country.
  • Legal/Professional Training - We organize high-quality professional training programmes and workshops for lawyers and professionals.
02

Intellectual Property

  • Trademarks, Patents, Copyrights
  • Industrial Design Protection
  • Trade Secrets & Domain Names
  • OAPI Countries Coverage

Full IP Services:

  • We protect the ownership of intellectual assets of organisations, brands, and businesses. These include trademarks, patents, copyrights, industrial design, trade secrets, trade names, domain name protection etc.
  • We offer intellectual property services both in Nigeria and Africa particularly including the African Intellectual Property Organization (OAPI) Countries.
  • OAPI coverage includes: Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, the Central African Republic, Chad, the Comoro Islands, Congo Brazzaville, Côte d'Ivoire, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Mauritania, Niger, Senegal, Togo etc.

We attend to intellectual property needs of our clientele with attendant expertise.

03

Real Estate

  • Property Due Diligence
  • Title Investigation & Transfer
  • Land Document Preparation
  • Real Estate Legal Advisory

Property Law Practice:

  • We are proficient in offering clients real estate legal services that include conducting property due diligence and title investigation before transaction.
  • Preparation of land title documents, procuring necessary land documents and other necessary property documents for our clients.
  • Real estate legal advisory to help you make informed property investment decisions.
